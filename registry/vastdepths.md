## Description

A content mod: data only, nothing to build. **Needs Vast Ores** (its ore hammer crushes these metals too).

| Metal | Where | Mined with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stellarite | deep overworld, y −64 to −32 | netherite |
| Infernium | the Nether, y 8 to 40 | stellarite |
| Voidium | the End, in end stone | infernium |

- Each metal has raw ore, raw block, ingot, nugget, block, dust, plate, gear and rod.
- A pickaxe, axe, shovel and sword of each (ingots and blaze rods), and a full armour set (ingots). All of it is unbreakable and stronger at each tier. Each has its own hand-drawn look, in the hand and worn.

| Tier | Sword | Axe | Mining speed | Armour (full set) | Toughness (full set) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Netherite (for comparison) | 8 | 10 | 9 | 20 | 12 |
| Stellarite | 12 | 14 | 12 | 24 | 16 |
| Infernium | 18 | 20 | 14 | 28 | 24 |
| Voidium | 30 | 32 | 16 | 34 | 32 |

- Three alloys, each from one ingot of two metals and a netherite ingot.
- With the Iron Furnaces port, the three metals make its top furnace tiers, named after them here: the Stellarite, Infernium and Voidium Furnaces and their upgrades.
